Every symbol in the edition, searchable by any English synonym or by BCI-AV
number, and filterable by part of speech.

Bliss encodes part of speech as the colour a symbol is drawn on, which is why
the filter reads `YELLOW`, `RED`, `BLUE` and so on rather than noun and verb.
